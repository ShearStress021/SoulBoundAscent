# Project Proposal: Dungeon Master Auto-Battler Strategy Game

## 1. High Concept

A single-player offline PC strategy game where the player is the Master, managing a town, summoning raw heroes, training them into combat roles, assigning squads, preparing items, and sending them into an automated tower dungeon.

The game combines:

- Auto-battler combat inspired by TFT / Auto Chess
- Hero training and squad selection inspired by Langrisser
- High-stakes tower survival inspired by Pick Me Up!
- Roster management, death, revival, and long-term consequences

The player does not directly control units during battle. Instead, the Master wins through preparation: roster decisions, job training, squad choice, deployment formation, item setup, town upgrades, and risk management.

## 2. Core Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| Strategic Preparation | The Master's main decisions happen before battle: who to train, deploy, equip, and support. |
| High-Stakes Roster Management | Heroes can die, be revived, inherited, or permanently lost. Losses affect the tower. |
| Deterministic Challenge | Floors are fixed and fair. Player experience changes through strategy, not difficulty sliders. |
| Town-Based Progression | The town controls training, level caps, equipment, items, revival, and recovery. |
| Auto-Battler Clarity | Battles resolve automatically, but the player can pause to inspect and understand outcomes. |

## 3. Core Gameplay Loop

1. Return to town.
2. Review next floor or invasion threat.
3. Summon raw heroes if resources allow.
4. Train heroes into jobs.
5. Select squad type for each deployed hero.
6. Enhance or repair personal equipment.
7. Craft/equip auto-trigger consumables.
8. Choose restaurant buff.
9. Deploy heroes on the grid.
10. Start automated battle.
11. Pause during battle only to inspect.
12. Resolve victory, defeat, deaths, equipment locks, rewards, and Tower Malice.
13. Continue climbing or defend invaded floors.

## 4. Combat Format

### Battle Type

Combat is a grid-based auto battler.

- Player places heroes before battle.
- Battle resolves automatically.
- Master can pause anytime to inspect units.
- No direct commands during combat.
- No manual item activation during combat.

### Battle Modes

| Mode | Description |
|---|---|
| Subjugation | Attempt to clear a new tower floor. |
| Protection | Defend a previously cleared floor from invasion. |

### MVP Board

Recommended MVP board:

```text
5 columns x 6 rows
```

- Player deployment area: bottom rows
- Enemy area: top rows
- Middle area: contested space
- Some floors may include hazard or blocked tiles

## 5. Heroes, Jobs, And Squads

### Summoning

Summoning should reduce RNG frustration.

Instead of summoning a fully random class hero, the Summoning Gate gives:

- Star rating
- Innate attribute
- Raw/untrained hero

The Master later chooses which job the hero trains into.

Example:

```text
Summoned Hero:
Star: 3
Innate Attribute: Flame
State: Untrained
```

### Hero Lifecycle

```text
Summoned Soul
-> Untrained Hero
-> Trained Hero
-> Deployed Hero
-> Downed Soul
-> Revived / Inherited / Permanently Lost
```

### Untrained Heroes

Untrained heroes can be deployed in emergencies, but:

- No squad
- No job skill
- No personal equipment
- Weaker battlefield impact

If an untrained hero gains levels before choosing a job, they receive minor bonus stats when trained.

### MVP Jobs

| Job | Role |
|---|---|
| Warrior | Frontline physical damage |
| Guardian | Tank/protector |
| Archer | Ranged physical damage |
| Mage | Magical damage |
| Healer | Healing/support |

Commander is a strong future addition but should be stretch for MVP.

### Squad System

Each trained hero chooses one compatible squad before battle.

MVP rules:

- 2 squad choices per job
- Squad level follows hero level
- Squad adds stats
- Squad grants one automatic skill or passive
- Squad is not simulated as separate soldiers

Example:

| Job | Squad A | Squad B |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Swordsmen: balanced damage | Duelists: higher crit, lower defense |
| Guardian | Shield Guard: defense | Spearmen: frontline control |
| Archer | Bowmen: faster attacks | Crossbowmen: armor pierce |
| Mage | Fire Circle: AOE/burn | Frost Circle: slow/debuff |
| Healer | Clerics: stronger heal | Acolytes: faster smaller heal |

## 6. Synergies

Add lightweight auto-chess-style synergies.

MVP synergy rules:

- Only 2 categories: Job Synergy and Innate Attribute Synergy
- Only 2-unit thresholds for MVP
- Small readable bonuses
- Clear deployment UI

Example job synergies:

| Synergy | Requirement | Bonus |
|---|---:|---|
| Warriors | 2 Warriors | Warriors gain physical attack |
| Guardians | 2 Guardians | Guardians gain defense |
| Archers | 2 Archers | Archers gain range or attack speed |
| Mages | 2 Mages | Mages gain magical attack |
| Healers | 2 Healers | Healing increased |

Example attribute synergies:

| Attribute | Requirement | Bonus |
|---|---:|---|
| Flame | 2 Flame heroes | Team gains damage |
| Stone | 2 Stone heroes | Team gains HP |
| Gale | 2 Gale heroes | Team gains attack speed |
| Light | 2 Light heroes | Team gains healing received |
| Shadow | 2 Shadow heroes | Team gains critical chance |

Avoid squad synergies in MVP.

## 7. Combat Math Direction

### Core Stats

| Stat | Function |
|---|---|
| HP | Health pool |
| Physical Attack | Physical damage |
| Magical Attack | Magical damage |
| Physical Defense | Flat physical reduction |
| Magical Defense | Flat magical reduction |
| Physical Armor | Percent physical reduction |
| Magical Armor | Percent magical reduction |
| Attack Speed | Attack frequency |
| Movement Speed | Grid movement timing |
| Critical Chance | Chance to crit |
| Critical Pierce | Amount of defense/armor bypassed on crit |

### Damage Formula

```text
Reduced Damage = Incoming Damage * (1 - Relevant Armor%)
Final Damage = max(1, Reduced Damage - Relevant Defense)
```

Critical hits bypass part of armor and defense based on Critical Pierce.

Healing:

- Restores HP
- Can crit
- Crit healing increases heal amount

### Type Advantage

For MVP, use job/type advantage, not elemental combat advantage.

Elemental/innate attributes should mainly affect growth, training affinity, and synergies.

Recommended MVP triangle:

```text
Warrior beats Archer
Archer beats Mage
Mage beats Guardian
Guardian beats Warrior
Healer is neutral/support
```

Suggested values:

```text
Advantage: +20% damage
Disadvantage: -10% damage
```

## 8. Town Systems

The town is the Master's strategic base.

### MVP Buildings

| Building | Function |
|---|---|
| Town Hall | Town level, hero level cap, building unlocks |
| Summoning Gate | Summon raw heroes |
| Training Facility | Choose job and provide passive XP |
| Blacksmith | Enhance, repair, and unlock personal equipment |
| Shrine | Revive souls, inheritance, emergency recovery |
| Apothecary | Craft auto-trigger consumables |
| Restaurant | One pre-battle food buff |
| Barracks | Basic squad upgrades |

Specialized squad buildings can be added later.

### Town Level

Town level controls:

- Hero level cap
- Building unlocks
- Training limits
- Equipment enhancement cap
- Squad upgrade cap

Final hero level cap:

```text
Final Cap = min(Star Cap, Town Level Cap)
```

## 9. Equipment

Each trained hero has fixed personal equipment.

The Master does not swap gear. Instead, the Master can:

- Enhance equipment
- Repair equipment
- Eventually awaken equipment

MVP rules:

- Equipment unlocks when hero chooses a job
- Equipment is fixed to the hero
- Equipment can be locked on a failed floor
- Equipment can be recovered by clearing the floor
- If hero is permanently lost, equipment can be salvaged after recovery

Equipment states:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Available | Hero can use equipment |
| Locked | Equipment is trapped on a floor |
| Recovered | Equipment returns after floor clear |
| Salvageable | Hero is gone; equipment can be dismantled |

## 10. Items And Restaurant Buffs

### Consumables

Items are equipped before battle and trigger automatically.

MVP item limit:

```text
1 consumable per deployed hero
```

Example items:

| Item | Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Elixir | HP <= 70% | Heal + regeneration |
| Cleansing Herb | Debuff lands | Remove debuff and grant resistance |
| Barrier Charm | Battle start | Grant shield |

### Restaurant

Restaurant provides one temporary pre-battle buff.

Examples:

| Meal | Effect |
|---|---|
| Warrior Stew | Frontline heroes gain HP |
| Hunter Skewers | Backline heroes gain attack |
| Focus Tea | First skill cooldown reduced |
| Blessed Soup | Healing received increased |

Only one meal should be active per dungeon attempt.

## 11. Death, Revival, And Permanent Loss

### Subjugation Outcomes

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Partial win | Floor cleared, dead heroes become revivable souls, equipment retrieved |
| Total loss | Floor uncleared, souls revivable, equipment locked until floor cleared |

### Protection Outcomes

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Partial win | Floor protected, dead heroes revivable, equipment retrieved |
| Failed defense | Floor occupied, souls/equipment locked until reclaimed |

### Shrine Revival

The Shrine can revive dead heroes before their souls expire.

Rules:

- Stronger heroes take longer to revive
- Stronger heroes also have longer soul expiry windows
- Revived heroes return without locked equipment if equipment is trapped
- Permanent death occurs when soul expires or inheritance is chosen

### Soul Inheritance

Instead of revival, the Shrine can transfer part of a dead hero's soul.

Inheritance:

- Dead hero is permanently lost
- Recipient gains XP
- Compatible recipient may gain minor permanent stats
- Tower Malice increases partially
- Faster than revival

MVP can simplify inheritance to XP-only.

## 12. Tower Malice And Invasion

### Tower Malice

Permanent hero loss feeds the tower and increases Tower Malice.

Malice should create pressure, not instant failure.

Possible MVP effects:

| Malice Level | Effect |
|---:|---|
| Low | Invasion pressure rises faster |
| Medium | Invasion enemies gain small bonus |
| High | Boss or invasion gains extra enemy |

### Invasion

If the Master stalls instead of climbing, upper floors begin invading cleared floors.

Rules:

- First invasion is weaker than the actual upper floor
- Invasions escalate if the player keeps stalling
- Invasion rewards are lower than new floor rewards
- If invasion reaches the homebase, town defense occurs
- Losing town defense causes game over

For MVP:

- One occupied floor at a time
- Simple invasion counter
- Town defense can be stretch if time is tight

## 13. Deployment Limits And Underdog Rewards

Each floor has a max hero deployment cap.

The Master may bring fewer heroes for bonus rewards.

Example:

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| Bring max heroes | Safer clear |
| Bring 1 fewer hero | Higher XP/reward |
| Bring too few heroes | Very risky, optional challenge |

MVP recommendation:

```text
Only support 1 under-deployment bonus tier.
```

Example:

```text
Bring 1 fewer hero than max: +25% XP
```

## 14. MVP Feature Lock

### MVP Must Include

| Feature | MVP Target |
|---|---|
| Floors | 5 handcrafted floors |
| Combat | Grid-based auto battle |
| Battle modes | Subjugation + basic Protection |
| Party size | 4 deployed heroes |
| Jobs | Warrior, Guardian, Archer, Mage, Healer |
| Squads | 2 squad options per job |
| Summoning | Star + innate attribute + untrained hero |
| Training | Choose job through town |
| Synergies | Job + attribute, 2-unit threshold |
| Equipment | Fixed personal equipment, enhance/locked/recovered |
| Items | 3 auto-trigger consumables |
| Town | Core buildings only |
| Shrine | Revival + simple inheritance |
| Death | Revivable soul, permanent death, Malice |
| Invasion | Basic escalating invasion pressure |
| Save/load | Basic local save |

### Stretch Features

| Feature | Notes |
|---|---|
| Commander job | Strong fit, but not required for MVP |
| Equipment awakening | Add after enhancement works |
| Enemy captured equipment bonuses | Cool but complex |
| Multi-floor invasions | Full-game feature |
| Deep inheritance stats/passives | Full-game feature |
| Specialized squad buildings | Full-game feature |
| Elemental combat triangle | Avoid until job triangle is stable |
| Multiple item slots | Later upgrade |
| Large hero pool | Later content expansion |

## 15. MVP Roadmap

### Month 1: Combat Prototype

- Grid deployment
- Auto movement and targeting
- Basic jobs and enemies
- HP/damage/death
- Pause inspection
- One test floor

Exit goal:

```text
Player can deploy units, start auto battle, inspect, and resolve win/loss.
```

### Month 2: Hero And Town Systems

- Summoning raw heroes
- Training into jobs
- Squad selection
- Basic town screen
- Personal equipment
- Save/load

Exit goal:

```text
Player can build a roster and prepare for floors through town systems.
```

### Month 3: Death, Invasion, And Content

- 5 floors
- Shrine revival
- Equipment locking/recovery
- Tower Malice
- Basic invasion/protection mode
- Consumable items
- Restaurant buff

Exit goal:

```text
Player can complete the full tower slice with losses and recovery.
```

### Month 4: Polish And Balance

- UI clarity
- Synergy panel
- Better battle feedback
- Balance pass
- Audio/VFX
- Tutorial prompts
- Bug fixing

Exit goal:

```text
A new player can understand and complete the demo without developer explanation.
```

## 16. Key Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Too many systems | Lock MVP tightly and defer stretch features |
| Auto battle feels unclear | Add pause inspection, clear UI, visible targeting, combat log |
| Death spiral | Add Shrine revival and one emergency recovery option |
| Economy imbalance | Use fixed floor rewards for MVP |
| Synergies become overwhelming | Only 2-unit job/attribute synergies |
| Equipment scope grows | Fixed personal equipment only, no loot inventory |
| Invasion too punishing | Start with one occupied floor and clear warnings |
| Squad system too large | 2 squad options per job, no separate squad XP |

## 17. Current Design Position

The project should be pitched as:

```text
A single-player dungeon auto-battler where the player acts as the Master:
summoning raw heroes, training them into jobs, assigning squads, upgrading
their personal equipment, preparing town resources, and sending them into a
dangerous tower where death, revival, inheritance, invasion, and Tower Malice
shape the campaign.
```

This is the strongest current direction because it has a clear identity, supports long-term strategy, and remains achievable if the MVP scope is controlled.
